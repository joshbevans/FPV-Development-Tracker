# FPV Development Tracker

A tool to track my improvements flying an FPV drone in the simulator, as well as a chance for me to learn some Blazor!

---

## Record your times!
Page to add completed runs, where you can select maps and courses as well as the drone used.  
![Record your times](https://github.com/joshbevans/FPV-Development-Tracker/blob/development/DemoImages/addCompletedRun.png)

---

## Track your records!
You can view your completed runs, and it will highlight your best runs. It also provides useful stats like time differences between attempts.  
![Track your records](https://github.com/joshbevans/FPV-Development-Tracker/blob/development/DemoImages/runHistory.png)

---

## Visualise your progression!
The tracker also allows you to visualise your improvement over time and attempts.

### Progression Over Time
See how your runs improve over time, helping you spot trends and progress.  
![Improvement Over Time](https://github.com/joshbevans/FPV-Development-Tracker/blob/development/DemoImages/graph1.png)

### Progression Over Attempt Count
Track your progression across individual attempts to understand consistency and growth.  
![Progression Over Attempt Count](https://github.com/joshbevans/FPV-Development-Tracker/blob/development/DemoImages/graph2.png)
